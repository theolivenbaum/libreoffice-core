Where Paperless Differs

All 946 documents in the sample corpus were rendered twice — once by headless LibreOffice, once by Paperless — and compared page by page at 150 dpi. 754 came out clean. These are the other 192: every one that shows a pixel defect or paginates differently, worst first, each with the divergent page side by side and a reading of what actually went wrong.

How to read this

Each entry shows LibreOffice on the left, Paperless on the right, at the page where the two diverge most. The order is severity: documents with a pixel defect first, ranked by how badly the worst page's structure diverges, then the documents whose pages match but whose page counts do not. Every reading below is a description of what the two images show — not a diagnosis of the code behind it.
